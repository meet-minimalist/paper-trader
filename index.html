How it works

Holds the 20 strongest trending stocks in the NIFTY Total Market, ranked on 9/7/6/3-month returns measured to 10 days ago. Exits on a trend break or a profit-tightening trailing stop.

Signals are generated on the close and filled at the next session's open. Costs use Zerodha delivery rates (STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp duty, GST, DP charges). This is paper trading only — no real orders are placed.

About the controls. Changing the index loads a genuinely separate portfolio, simulated from the same start date and capital. Opening capital and “performance since” only change how the numbers are presented: returns are the same at any balance, and a later start date re-bases the view rather than re-running the strategy — the holdings would differ had it actually begun then.
